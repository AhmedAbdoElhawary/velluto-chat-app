# Velluto

A private iPhone messenger: 1:1 and group chats, voice notes, media, 24-hour stories and calls. These are the words code, docs and reviews use for its concepts.

## Language

### People

**Account**:
The person signed in on this device, identified by their phone number and session.
_Avoid_: User, Me

**Profile**:
What contacts see of the Account: name, photo and about line.
_Avoid_: Bio, user info

**Contact**:
Another person the Account can chat with.
_Avoid_: Friend, user, peer

### Chats

**Chat**:
A thread of messages between the Account and one or more Contacts, either direct (1:1) or a Group.
_Avoid_: Conversation (only the name of the screen that shows a Chat), thread, room

**Group**:
A Chat of kind group: it has a title and members can be added to it. A Group with only two people in it is still a Group.
_Avoid_: Group chat as a separate thing, channel

**Story**:
A photo or a line of text shared with contacts that disappears after 24 hours.
_Avoid_: Moment (marketing copy only), status, post
